3D picking for Universal Robots. A URCap whose 3D Pick program node uses a depth camera on the wrist to find every part of the size you enter, number them in the pick order you choose, and move the tool to the grip on each one. Your program opens and closes the gripper.

Planning figures, not guarantees. The numbers below are deliberately conservative estimates derived from the software's limits, its programmed speeds and the camera's noise. This release is a beta and the figures have not been measured on a production cell. Test with your parts, gripper and layout before you commit to them.

Not for (limits of the depth camera)

  • Measuring parts or quality checks
  • Defect, surface or print inspection
  • Telling apart parts of nearly the same size
  • Thin or flat parts: sheets, washers, labels
  • Transparent, mirror-like or very dark parts
  • Bin picking: piled or leaning parts
  • Shapes other than boxes and upright cylinders
  • Direct sunlight on the work area
  • Driving the gripper or the place move

Capabilities

  • Finds parts by volume, from depth alone: boxes on any face, cylinders on end; the floor is fitted round each part.
  • Any position, any rotation. Fingers across the short side, or the long one.
  • Eight pick orders: by rows or by columns, from any corner.
  • Checks before it moves: reach by the arm's kinematics, finger room.
  • Says why not: near misses drawn in yellow with the reason.
  • Queues what it saw: no trip back to the picture point.
  • One program node: sets rs_pick_found, rs_pick_loc. No internet.
